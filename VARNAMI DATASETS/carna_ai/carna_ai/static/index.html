Scientific Transparency & Team Rule 4 Notice

Raga similarity percentages and confidence scores in VARNAMI are computed using directional transition matrices, pitch-class histograms, and scale grammar. They represent relative model estimates, not guaranteed absolute probabilities. As per project constraints, uncalibrated real-singing claims are avoided, and features clearly distinguish baseline synthetic grammar from expert-verified prayogas.
